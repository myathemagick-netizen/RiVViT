## What this changes for RiVViT

- **Creation, presentation and reception need separate records.** A release date is not necessarily the date or place of inspiration. Nevermind's production spans studios; its release does not justify a Seattle pin.
- **Preserve actual dates independently of epochs.** The 1845 Walden event belongs near the 1842 Romantic marker; the 1945 bombings sit within the narrative around the 1946 trough. Do not shift events to match those markers.
- **Keep intervals and uncertainty visible.** Year-only construction evidence cannot become a day-accurate astronomical comparison. A battle needs duration and geographic extent rather than a museum point.
- **Reported experiences have their own evidence status.** Perpignan's history provides a date and station for Dalí's account. It does not establish the MMS explanation for that experience.

The June 1931 exhibition provides a useful bound on the painting's creation and does not support a September 1931 completion. The current July 1 wave reference remains a provisional calibration; this research does not change it automatically.

## Before rendering pins

Place names and roles are researched, but coordinates/footprints have not been independently verified: every geometry is deliberately null. Resolve historical venue locations, source coordinates, and label regional extents before drawing pins or calculating riftline distances. The JSON's open questions identify the remaining work per event. An unknown creation location and a distributed release should stay distinct from a verified point location.

The specialist Nevermind session dates need stronger corroboration before day-specific creative-process comparisons. Other named MMS candidates—Wilson's Sirius period, McKenna's La Chorrera episode, PKD's experiences, the Babalon Working, Roerich's route, punk and hyperpop—need their own source work. Private Camp Hero observations need an author-supplied date/location record and publication preferences. No private document or full MMS text is included here.
