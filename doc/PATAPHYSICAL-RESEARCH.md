# RiVViT's pattern research

Recorded from the project author's clarification, 2026-10-13.

RiVViT seeks patterns within the Myathe Magick System's pataphysics. Scientific proof of chronon pressure is not the project's objective. Astronomical calculations supply a consistent reference against which interpretations of the one available historical record can be mapped in different ways.

The author regards the present correspondence between novelty-wave timing and Saturn's movement, orbital position, rapid rotation, north-pole orientation, and changing Earth–Saturn distance as suggesting that the proposed pattern has validity within this framework. The current calculations cover orbital and pole geometry, distance, and a hypothetical travel model; they do not yet model rotation-driven emission or intensity.

Historical inspiration includes more than events judged positive. In the author's interpretation, dextrogyre events may seek to seal the dextrogyre shell and “repair the leaks” of the chronon dam. Event significance, polarity, geography, and dating need explicit records rather than a single positive/negative score. Destructive events and creative flourishing can be compared without assuming either category has one universal interpretation.

The working timeline is approximately the last 200 years, currently 1830–2026, because this period offers more collected data for comparing patterns. This is a practical data boundary, not an assertion that the model begins in 1830.

## Proposed historical layers

- Electromagnetic anomalies over time.
- Wars and related events.
- Inventions and places where artistic ideas flourish.
- Religious movements and their spread.
- Animal migration patterns.
- Weather patterns.
- Geological events, including earthquakes along faults.

These are proposed layers, not datasets already implemented. Future records should preserve sources, date precision/ranges, locations and uncertainty, geographic extent, and the author's interpretations separately. Layers should share the timeline and permit multiple interpretations of the same history. Absence of a record should not count as absence of an event. Collection density and changing reporting practices should remain visible when comparing periods.

## Sirius

Sirius belongs in the observatory because of its cultural interest and possible place in this pataphysical interpretation. Compare its changing distance and A/B orbital cycle with the same history. A possible different force, strength, or propagation speed remains open; the current novelty wave does not calculate intensity. Reusing the Saturn fit is a scenario for scale comparison, not a required common force law. Cultural associations should be documented by culture and source when those records are added.
